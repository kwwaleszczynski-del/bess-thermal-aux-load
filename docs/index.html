Container thermal balance, HVAC auxiliary load and net RTE of a grid battery in Poland

Two-node thermal and auxiliary-power model of a generic 20-foot 2.5 MW / 5 MWh LFP battery container, run hour by hour (15-minute sub-steps) on ERA5 weather for Central Poland. It follows the energy from the cells through the PCS and the 0.8/15 kV transformer to the point of common coupling (PCC), with cell losses, chiller COP, heating, pumps or fans and the control base load. All inputs are stated assumptions for a generic container, not data of a specific product. Code, inputs and method: github.com/kwwaleszczynski-del/bess-thermal-aux-load. Companion operational reporting: bess-ops-warranty.

7. Checks

Each check can fail. The energy balance is bookkeeping: every electrical loss has to reappear as stored heat or as heat leaving the plant. No measured container data are used; the model is not validated against field measurements.
