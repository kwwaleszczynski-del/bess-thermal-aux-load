Container thermal balance, HVAC auxiliary load and net RTE of a grid battery in Poland

Coupled electro-thermal and auxiliary-power model of a 20-foot 2.5 MW / 5.0 MWh LFP battery container operating across an 8,760-hour Polish meteorological year (52.2 N, 21.0 E). Models cell internal Joule and reversible entropic heat, a two-node thermal state-space comparing liquid cold-plate cooling against rooftop forced-air DX units, Carnot-derated chiller COP, winter PTC heating below 12 degC, inverter switching/conduction losses, and 0.8/15 kV step-up transformer core and copper losses. Companion operational reporting repository: bess-ops-warranty. Code and method: github.com/kwwaleszczynski-del/bess-thermal-aux-load.

Financial models routinely assume a flat 88 % AC-AC round-trip efficiency. Once MV transformer iron core loss, continuous BMS/controls draw, summer chiller load, and winter anti-plating heating are metered at the 15 kV point of common coupling (PCC), net RTE averages 84.1 % at 1.0 cycle/day (dropping to 79.4 % in January) and falls to 73.8 % under 0.35 cycles/day capacity-market reserve duty.
